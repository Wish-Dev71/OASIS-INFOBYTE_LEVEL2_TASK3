Who Was Abdul Sattar Edhi?

Abdul Sattar Edhi was born on 1 January 1928 in Bantva, Gujarat, British India. After the partition of India in 1947, he moved to Pakistan and settled in Karachi.

Witnessing poverty and suffering inspired him to dedicate his life to helping others. He began with a small dispensary and gradually built a humanitarian network that served people regardless of their religion, background, or financial status.

Alongside his wife, Bilquis Edhi, he supported ambulance services, shelters, orphanages, clinics, and other welfare initiatives. His simple lifestyle reflected his belief that service to humanity matters more than personal wealth.
